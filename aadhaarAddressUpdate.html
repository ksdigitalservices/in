Labour License FAQ

What is a labour license?
A labour license is a mandatory registration for contractors employing workers under contract labour law.

Who needs labour license?
Contractors employing 20 or more workers must obtain labour license.

How long does approval take?
Usually between 7 and 30 working days.

Can labour license be renewed?
Yes, labour licenses must be renewed periodically depending on validity period.
