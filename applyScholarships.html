Common Problems Students Face While Applying Scholarships

Students often lose scholarship opportunities because of avoidable mistakes during the application process. Scholarship portals require accurate details and correct document formats.

  • Incorrect file format while uploading income certificate.

Documents Required for Scholarship Application

  • Income certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Bonafide certificate
  • Previous academic marks memo
  • Bank account details

Submitting valid documents is essential for successful scholarship approval. Incorrect or expired documents are one of the most common reasons for rejection.
