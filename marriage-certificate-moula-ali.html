Why Marriage Certificate is Important

A marriage certificate is an official legal document that confirms the marital status of a couple. It is often required for passport applications, visa processing, bank nominee updates, insurance claims, and property matters.

Many government and legal procedures require proof of marriage. Without a registered marriage certificate, couples may face difficulties when dealing with legal or administrative matters.
