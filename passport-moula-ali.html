How to Apply Passport Online in India

The passport application process in India can be completed online through the official Passport Seva portal. Applicants must create an account, fill the passport form, upload documents and schedule an appointment at Passport Seva Kendra (PSK).

Step 1: Register on Passport Seva Portal
Step 2: Fill Passport Application Form
Step 3: Upload Required Documents
Step 4: Pay Passport Fee
Step 5: Book PSK Appointment
Step 6: Attend Biometric Verification
Step 7: Police Verification & Passport Delivery

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does passport processing take?

Passport processing normally takes 20–30 days after police verification.

How to apply passport online?

What documents are required for passport?
