Passport Application Assistance in Hyderabad

A passport is one of the most important identity documents issued by the Government of India. It allows citizens to travel internationally and serves as an official proof of identity and nationality. Many individuals in Moula Ali, ECIL, AS Rao Nagar and nearby Hyderabad areas require passport services for travel, employment, education, or immigration purposes.

The passport application process involves online registration, form submission, appointment booking at Passport Seva Kendra (PSK), biometric verification, and police verification. Many applicants face delays because of incorrect information or document mismatches during the application process.

Our passport assistance service helps applicants complete the entire process correctly. We guide you through document verification, online application submission, and appointment booking so that your passport application proceeds smoothly.

Types of Passport Applications

New Passport Application

Apply for a passport for the first time with complete online application assistance.

Passport Renewal

Renew expired passports or update information such as address or marital status.

Tatkal Passport

Urgent passport processing under the Tatkal scheme with faster appointment scheduling.

Documents Required for Passport Application

  • Aadhaar card
  • Voter ID card
  • Bank passbook
  • Electricity bill
  • Birth certificate or SSC certificate

Providing correct documents is essential because mismatched or incomplete documents may cause application rejection.

Passport Application Process

Step 1: Register on passport portal.
Step 2: Fill passport application form.
Step 3: Pay passport application fee.
Step 4: Book PSK appointment.
Step 5: Attend biometric verification.
Step 6: Police verification and passport delivery.

Common Passport Application Mistakes

  • Incorrect spelling of name
  • Address mismatch with Aadhaar
  • Wrong passport category selection
  • Uploading incorrect documents

Avoiding these mistakes can significantly reduce delays in passport processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does passport processing take?

Normal passport processing usually takes 20–30 days depending on police verification.

Is PSK appointment mandatory?

Yes biometric verification at Passport Seva Kendra is compulsory.

Can passport details be corrected?

Yes corrections can be made during passport renewal or reissue application.
