How to Apply Passport Online in India

Applicants can apply for a passport online through the Passport Seva portal. The online process includes registration, form submission, document upload and appointment booking at Passport Seva Kendra.

Step 1: Register on Passport Seva portal
Step 2: Fill passport application form
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Pay passport application fee
Step 5: Book PSK appointment
Step 6: Attend biometric verification
Step 7: Police verification and passport delivery

Documents Required for Passport

Submitting correct documents is essential for successful passport processing. Applicants must provide identity proof, address proof and date of birth proof.

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Electricity Bill
  • Birth Certificate
  • SSC Certificate

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does passport processing take?

Normal passport processing usually takes 20–30 days depending on police verification.

Can passport details be corrected?

Yes corrections can be made during passport renewal or reissue application.

Do I need PSK appointment?

Yes all applicants must attend biometric verification at Passport Seva Kendra.
