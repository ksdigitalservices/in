Common Mistakes That Cause Passport Rejection

  • Mismatch between Aadhaar and application address.
  • Incorrect date of birth entry.
  • Uploading unclear or invalid documents.
  • Booking wrong passport category (normal vs Tatkal).

These mistakes result in rescheduling, extra visits to PSK, and additional delays. Structured assistance prevents these errors.

Documents Required for Passport Application

Address Proof

Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Bank Passbook, Electricity Bill.

Date of Birth Proof

Birth Certificate, SSC Certificate, PAN Card (as applicable).

Providing correct and updated documents is critical. Expired or mismatched documents lead to objections.

Step-by-Step Passport Application Process

Step 1: Verify eligibility and required documents.
Step 2: Register and fill passport application form online.
Step 3: Pay government fee and book PSK appointment.
Step 4: Visit Passport Seva Kendra for biometric verification.
Step 5: Police verification and passport delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does passport processing take?

Normal passport processing usually takes 20–30 days depending on police verification. Tatkal processing may take 3–7 days after appointment.

Is PSK appointment mandatory?

Yes. Biometric verification and document verification at Passport Seva Kendra is compulsory.

Can I correct mistakes after submission?

Corrections are limited after submission. Errors often require fresh application.

Do you guarantee passport approval?

Approval depends on government verification. We assist in submitting accurate applications to reduce rejection risk.
