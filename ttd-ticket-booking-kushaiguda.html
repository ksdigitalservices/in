TTD ₹300 Special Entry Darshan – Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ₹300 Special Entry Darshan?

₹300 Special Entry Darshan is a paid darshan ticket provided by TTD that allows devotees to have faster access to Lord Venkateswara darshan at Tirumala.

2. How can I book ₹300 Special Entry Darshan tickets?

Tickets can be booked online through the official TTD website or through authorized service centers.

3. How many tickets can be booked in one login?

Usually up to 6 tickets can be booked in a single transaction using one TTD account.

4. How many days in advance are tickets released?

TTD normally releases ₹300 darshan tickets one month in advance through the official portal.

5. What ID proof is required for darshan?

Devotees must carry valid ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, or driving license during darshan entry.

6. Can I change the darshan date after booking?

No, once booked the darshan date and time cannot usually be changed.

7. Is cancellation allowed for ₹300 darshan tickets?

TTD generally does not allow cancellation or refund for Special Entry Darshan tickets.

8. What is the reporting time for darshan?

Devotees must report at the specified entry point at least 1–2 hours before the booked darshan slot.

9. Are children required to book a darshan ticket?

Children below 12 years are usually allowed free entry along with parents, but valid age proof may be required.
